How to sweep

Walk over the lotus to gather it. Flowers in your arms are worth nothing — carry them back to the black ship on the shore and stow them, and only then do they count.

The more you are carrying when you stow, the better the rate: every five flowers in one armful adds ×0.5, up to ×3. So hoard. That is the trap, and it is the whole game.

A white lotus is worth 10, a gold one 30. Stowing also clears about a third of your drowse, so the ship is your brake as well as your till.

The deep end of the meadow grows gold lotus, worth three of the white and more than twice as strong. Nothing good is far from the ship by accident.

What the lotus does

Every flower you pick raises your drowse. As it rises the island stops behaving.

Sweet — the ground breathes. Drifting — your feet no longer go where you point them, and the meadow is slippery underfoot. Lotus-eyed — flowers appear that are not there, and your fingers start closing on air. Forgetting — there are three ships on this beach and two of them are lies.

At 100 you sit down among the flowers and stay. That is the end of the run, and everything in your arms is lost.

Two things clear your head: the surf — wade the wash at the top of the beach and it drains fast, though the water drags at your legs — and stowing, which takes about a third off it, because you have to look at the ship to do it.

What Homer says

Nine days off course from Cape Malea, Odysseus lands among the Lotophagoi and sends three men inland to ask who lives there. The islanders are not hostile. They offer lotus, and that is the whole attack.

Anyone who tasted the honey-sweet fruit lost all wish to carry a message back or to leave at all. They wanted only to stay there cropping lotus and let the homecoming go.

Odysseus went in after them, dragged them back to the ships weeping, and had them tied under the benches — which is why, if the glass runs out while your arms are full, you get a quarter of what you were holding and no thanks for it.

The scoring follows the poem: what you actually save is the nostos, the homecoming, and it is paid out of however clear your head is at the end.
